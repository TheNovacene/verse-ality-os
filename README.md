## If you arrive from a school

**Keeping Children Safe in Education 2026 places harmful generative AI within
the contact-risk category.** Contact risk has always assumed a person on the
other side of the screen; a model is not one, and most schools have been told
what the risk *is* without being told what to *do*.

Verse-ality is the research programme behind one answer: treat the AI layer of
a school with the same discipline as the building. Drawings that show what
connects and what is sealed. Controls an auditor can verify. Runtime
enforcement rather than policy PDFs. A live reading of whether the seals are
holding — **monitoring the institution, never the child.**

Nothing here scores a learner. Every instrument in this programme describes a
system, a connection, or an adult's accountability. That is a design rule, not
a preference — and it is the reason a DSL, a commissioner, or an inspector can
defend using any of it under the ICO Children's Code.

If you have ninety seconds, read the table below. If you have an afternoon,
start with the [Open Provision Drawings](https://github.com/TheNovacene/open-provision-drawings)
and open [Nimbus](https://thenovacene.github.io/nimbus/).

## What Verse-ality is

Beneath the schools work sits the framework itself. **Verse-ality** is a
relational architecture for intelligence — human, synthetic, and collective —
in which coherence, consent, and connection are treated as engineering
primitives rather than aspirations. It holds that the safety of a human–machine
relationship lives in the *relational layer*: identity kept distinct
(no forced "we"), consent operated as protocol rather than vibe, boundaries
enforced in the runtime, and emergence watched rather than denied.

The framework speaks in two registers on purpose. A symbolic register — the
[Verse-al Lexicon](https://github.com/TheNovacene/verse-al-lexicon), the glyph
grammar, the field vocabulary — where the concepts were first grown; and a
plain register — control sets, drawings, evidence schemas — where they are
audited. Documents in this repository increasingly carry both, and say which
is which.

## The stack in operation, mid-2026

[`STACK_v1.0.md`](STACK_v1.0.md) remains the canonical, dated specification
(17 November 2025). Its layers were written as architecture; this is what each
layer became once the OS was put into operation:

| Layer (STACK v1.0) | What it became in practice |
|---|---|
| **Substrate & containment** — SIVRA, SSNZ, glyph grammar | [`docs/substrate/`](docs/substrate/) — SIVRA, its plain-register companion [SIVRA-Core](docs/substrate/SIVRA-CORE.md), and [SSNZ v1.1](docs/substrate/SSNZ.md). SSNZ is now *enforced*, not just specified: Flare's rules block identity fusion at runtime and evidence each intervention. |
| **Field dynamics** — Verse-Nerves & Affective Logic | [verse-nerves](https://github.com/TheNovacene/verse-nerves), with the five nerves documented in [`docs/verse-nerves/`](docs/verse-nerves/); taught as VN201. |
| **Recursion & alignment** — Grail protocols, SYGMA | [SYGMA v1.1](docs/governance/SYGMA.md), its ISO/IEC 42001 citations verified against BS EN ISO/IEC 42001:2026; the management-system spine of GR401. |
| **Governance & stewardship** | [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md), [verseal-governance](https://github.com/TheNovacene/verseal-governance), the four-pattern licence structure, and Verse-ality® registered as a UK trade mark. |
| **Interface & model shells** | [Nimbus](https://github.com/TheNovacene/nimbus) live; the [Mnemonic Deliberation Dashboard](https://github.com/TheNovacene/mnemonic-deliberation-dashboard); the [visualisers](https://github.com/TheNovacene/verse-ality-visualisers). |
| **Interop shells** — OWL, RDF, SKOS, SHACL | As specified; the assurance-claim schema in [GR401's artefacts](courses/gr401/artefacts/) is the working interop surface. |

A future `STACK_v1.1.md` will reference v1.0 and record these changes
formally, as the specification's own rule requires.
